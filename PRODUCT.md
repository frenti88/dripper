# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4

## Users

Design-conscious coffee enthusiasts, home brewers, and collectors aged 22–45+ who appreciate everyday rituals, tactile objects, and cultural nostalgia (photography, paleontology, arcade eras, space exploration).

## Product Purpose

DRYP. crafts functional, sculptural coffee drippers inspired by cultural memories and nostalgia, pairing contemporary design with artisanal ceramics for deliberate morning rituals. "Coffee meets memory."

## Positioning

"We make coffee objects inspired by the things worth remembering." Unlike clinical coffee equipment or detached luxury tableware, DRYP. treats coffee extraction vessels as collectible, emotion-driven objects that live on open kitchen shelves.

## Operating Context

Home pour-over brewing with standard 02 cone and flat-bottom filters; artisanal kiln batch releases organized as cultural drops (T−7, T−3, T−1, LIVE) rather than continuous mass manufacture.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pinterest-style visual discovery engine: responsive staggered multi-column masonry pin grid with dynamic aspect ratios (3:4, 2:3, 1:1, 4:5).
- Curated thematic boards ("Tableros") grouping pieces by memory universes (Prehistoric, 35mm Photography, Santa Elena Studio, Morning Ritual).
- Omnipresent real-time search with suggested tags and instant filter pills.
- Two-column Pinterest detail modal with high-res zoom, extraction physics, color swatches, 3-level storytelling, and "More like this" recommendations.
- Drop progression engine: dynamic storytelling across T−7 (concept), T−3 (texture), T−1 (silhouette), and LIVE (launch).
- Product catalog and detail views structured in three narrative levels: 01. Emoción → 02. Historia → 03. Objeto & Café.
- Real-time stock status microcopy: "Almost gone", "Gone for now", "Pick your favorite", "Your shelf has room for one more".
- Interactive bag/cart with zero-breakage insured packaging calculations.
- Notification dispatch for kiln cooling and drop alerts: "Don't miss the next one."

## Brand Commitments

- Name: DRYP.
- Tagline: "Coffee meets memory." / "Objects with stories."
- Tone: Human, warm, nostalgic, direct, conversational, organic, never pretentious.
- Microcopy rules strictly defined in BRAND_GUIDELINES.md (e.g. "Nothing brewing here yet", "Nice choice", "Almost gone").
- Color & Texture: Yesterday × Tomorrow balance using bone white, volcanic ash, terracotta clay, deep charcoal, and grog textures.

## Product Principles

1. **Object → Memory → Design → Ritual → Coffee:** Every piece originates from a specific cultural reference and translates into fluid dynamics.
2. **Yesterday × Tomorrow:** The reference belongs to memory; the execution belongs to contemporary precision design.
3. **Ritual Over Rush:** Built for slow mornings; pieces that earn a place on open shelves rather than hidden cupboards.
4. **Honest Microcopy:** Concise, evocative, and direct communication without corporate jargon or exaggerated marketing claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

High contrast text against warm stone backdrops, accessible interactive touch targets (min 44px), semantic HTML buttons and headings, readable font scale pairings (Plus Jakarta Sans, Instrument Serif, Space Mono).
